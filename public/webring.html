Bolognese

Bolognese is my webring, it works like a standard webring. To join fill in the form with your wanted name and your website link, your name should be alphanumeric. When putting the embed on your site, make sure to replace [NAME] with the same name you put in the form.

Rules / Information:

  • I do not accept any racism, transphobia, or bigotry.
  • I do not accept sites that are specifically explicit / NSFW sites. However you are allowed pages with artistic nudity as long as the page is marked explicit, or the image is marked explicit and blurred until hovered or similar.
  • Your site must have actual content.
  • You need to add the embed to your own site if you are added to the webring to keep it unbroken.

Embed

Code:

<iframe src="https://spagwhetti.github.io/embed.html?name=[NAME]" style="border:none; height:3rem; width:100%"></iframe>
